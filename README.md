# My High School Awards and Achievements

Hi! My name is Vishant Baskar. This page shares some of the things I have done during high school. I am interested in STEM, aerospace, computer science, art, leadership, and languages.

## Honors

- **Business Professionals of America (BPA):** I placed 11th at the state competition and 6th at the national competition. [Learn more](https://www.friscoisd.org/article/3004106)
- **Texas VASE:** I advanced to the state art event. [Learn more](https://www.friscoisd.org/o/lshs/article/3005985)
- **Change Bowl:** I won first place for sharing an idea that could make schools and communities better.
- **Eagle Scout:** I completed all the requirements and merit badges, including my final project. I am waiting for my board review.
- **Destination Imagination:** I competed with Team Carpediem, an independent team from Frisco, Texas.
  - **2025:** We qualified for Global Finals in the Technical Challenge and received the Magellan Award. [Learn more](https://www.nextregiondi.com/2025-special-awards)
  - **2024:** We placed third at Global Finals and received the Renaissance Award in the Technical Challenge. At the state competition, we placed first in the Instant Challenge and sixth in the Technical Challenge. At the regional competition, we placed first in the Technical and Fine Arts Challenges and third in the Service Challenge. [See the results](https://www.destinationimagination.org/wp-content/uploads/GF24-Results.pdf)
  - **2023:** We placed first at Global Finals in the Service and Instant Challenges. We also became World Champions in the Digital Open Challenge and received the Magellan Award and Renaissance Award. [See the results](https://www.destinationimagination.org/wp-content/uploads/GF23-Results.pdf)
